Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026. Jarvis is a personal, self-hosted assistant with a single user: its owner. This policy describes what it does with Google user data.

What Jarvis accesses

Only the owner's own Gmail accounts, which he signed in himself, with the Gmail API permission gmail.modify. Jarvis reads messages and their attachments, and changes labels on them: starring, its own "Jarvis/" labels, archiving, and marking as read.

What Jarvis never does

It never sends, forwards, drafts, trashes or permanently deletes email, and its software has no way to.

How the data is used and stored

Messages are processed and stored only on the owner's own server, at his home, to answer his questions and keep his records (bills, orders, deliveries). They are processed by a language model that runs on that same server. Gmail data is never sent to any third-party service, including AI model providers, and is never used to train any model.

Sharing

Gmail data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone. Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted by the owner on his own server.

Contact

Through the support email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
