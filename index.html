Jarvis

A personal assistant for one person, running on his own home server.

Jarvis is a private, self-hosted assistant. It is not a product or a service: it has a single user, its owner, and nobody else can sign in to it.

With its owner's permission, Jarvis reads his own Gmail inboxes so it can keep track of bills, orders and deliveries, and keeps his inbox tidy: it stars bills, adds its own labels, and archives newsletters. It never sends, forwards or deletes email.

Everything it reads stays on the owner's own server. See the privacy policy.
